## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Daily ticket intake

Day 1 SHALL start with 4 non-incident tickets. Each later day SHALL add 3–6 tickets in parallel mode and 2–4 in serial mode, each one an incident with probability min(0.12, 0.03 × (day − 1)), where day is the new day's number: 3% on day 2, 6% on day 3, 9% on day 4 and 12% from day 5 to day 20. The log SHALL record — 第 N 天開工，新進 M 張工單 —. The queue SHALL list tickets not currently running, sorted by deadline ascending and then KPI descending, with the header `<n> 張・依到期排序`; when empty it SHALL read 工單清空了。可以提早下班，把工時留給明天。

#### Scenario: First day

- **WHEN** a run starts
- **THEN** the queue holds 4 tickets and none is an incident

#### Scenario: Incident ramp in the first week

- **WHEN** the incident probability for a new day's tickets is evaluated
- **THEN** it matches the table

##### Example: incident probability by day

| Day | Probability |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 0.03 |
| 3 | 0.06 |
| 4 | 0.09 |
| 5 | 0.12 |
| 12 | 0.12 |
| 20 | 0.12 |

#### Scenario: Incident share on day 2

- **WHEN** 10,000 tickets are generated by the day-2 intake
- **THEN** the share of incidents is within ±0.01 of 0.03

#### Scenario: Queue order

- **WHEN** the queue holds A (due day 3, KPI 6), B (due day 2, KPI 3) and C (due day 3, KPI 10)
- **THEN** the order is B, C, A
