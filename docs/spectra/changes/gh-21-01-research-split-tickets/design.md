## Context

Tickets are generated by `makeIssue` in `public/js/state.js` (complexity 1–5, base tokens `BASE`, KPI `KPI`, due date, stack, client, sensitivity, large codebase, store review, trap). The dispatch panel (`dispatchPanel` in `public/js/view.js`) offers 派工, 自己手寫, 先讓 agent 評估架構 and, on a revealed trap, 找主管重新評估. Architecture evaluation (`evaluate` in `public/js/actions.js`) already models a paid pre-work agent action: `evalCost` gives tokens and hours, `charge` bills it, `auditRoll` and `checkOverdraft` apply penalties, `useHw` records machine use, and in parallel mode it advances the clock while marking the ticket running. A merge conflict (`settle`) already rewrites a ticket in place into a smaller follow-up ticket. Token estimates come from `est` in `public/js/calc.js`, which `presetBlock` (one-click dispatch) also uses.

The user settled these choices in `/spectra-discuss`: the ticket is visible on the card and research always splits it; research can be done by an agent or by the player; complexity 4 splits into 2 and 3, complexity 5 into 3 and 3, KPI total unchanged; direct dispatch only multiplies tokens (2.5 at first, 4 after measurement, see Values tuned after the simulator run). The remaining defaults (rates, costs, interactions) were proposed in discuss and accepted by invoking propose.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- A visible research ticket type with a clear choice: research then split, or dispatch directly at ×4 tokens.
- Agent research reuses the architecture-evaluation charging path; self research reuses hand-writing's blocking rule.
- Splitting yields two independent, ordinary tickets that keep the original's deadline, owner fields and total reward.

**Non-Goals:**

- No hidden research tickets and no chance that research fails to split (rejected in discuss: overlaps trap tickets).
- No capability-dependent split quality (rejected option 明牌＋研究看模型能力).
- No penalty on success rate or run time for direct dispatch (rejected options).
- No new penalty on hand-writing a research ticket: complexity 4–5 hand-writing already needs 8.8h–11h, more than a day.
- Reasoning effort, MCP docs and SDD do not change research cost; research does not count as an engineering investment.
- No recursive splitting; parts are never research tickets.
- No change to the month-end score formula or grade thresholds.

## Decisions

### Research ticket generation in makeIssue

`makeIssue` sets `research=true` when the ticket is not an incident, complexity is 4 or 5, and a roll is below `RESEARCH_RATE` (0.3). The roll is made only when `RESEARCH_RATE>0` and complexity is 4–5, after every existing random draw, so `SIM_RESEARCH_RATE=0` keeps the random sequence and fixed-seed simulator output identical to the code before this change. A research ticket takes its title from `STACKS[stack].pool.research`, an array of `{t, parts:[a, b]}`, and stores `parts` on the ticket. Every stack including `fe` gets at least 3 entries written in the same short, concrete style as existing titles. `RESEARCH_RATE` lives in `state.js` next to `TRAP_RATE` with a `setResearchRate` setter for the simulator.

Alternative considered: deriving part titles as 「<原標題>（上）／（下）」 — rejected, the design doc requires concrete titles and the discuss preview showed named parts.

### Direct-dispatch token premium in est

`est` multiplies `tk` by `RESEARCH_DIRECT_TK` (4, in `data.js`) when `is.research`. Because `lo`, `hi`, the cost line, `presetBlock`'s quota and wallet checks and `makeJob` all derive from `est`, the premium reaches the panel, one-click dispatch and the actual run with no other change. Hours and success rate are untouched.

### Research actions alongside evaluate

New `research(via)` in `public/js/actions.js` with `via` `agent` or `self`, and constants `RESEARCH_TK=40`, `RESEARCH_HRS=0.5`, `RESEARCH_SELF_HRS=1.5` in `data.js`.

- Agent: refused when the ticket is not a research ticket, `gigBlocked`, `hwBlock`, the local GPU is busy for local billing, or hours `RESEARCH_HRS × M.speed × localSpeed(v)` exceed the hours left. Model is the base model (no reasoning effort), tokens `RESEARCH_TK × M.verb`. Clock handling, `charge`, `useHw`, token statistics, `auditRoll` and `checkOverdraft` follow `evaluate`. When `charge` reports a quota shortfall the ticket is not split and the log says 額度不夠，研究沒做完.
- Self: refused when `manualBlocked()` or hours `RESEARCH_SELF_HRS × (unfamiliar ? UNFAMILIAR_HRS : 1)` exceed the hours left; no tokens, no audit.
- `researchBlock(is, via)` returns the refusal reason (or an empty string) and is shared by the action and the panel buttons, so a button is disabled exactly when the action would refuse: for agent research it checks outage, client ban, a missing machine, billing not usable (this covers company billing on an outsourced ticket), a busy local GPU and hours; for self research it checks blocked hand-writing and hours. A running research ticket returns 這張單正在跑, a normal ticket 不是研究單. This is broader than the evaluate button's `blocked` flag, which does not check billing or hours.

`canEvaluate` gains `!is.research`.

Alternative considered: one button with a modal choosing agent or self — rejected, two buttons match the existing panel pattern and need no new modal.

### Splitting replaces the ticket in place

`splitResearch(is)` removes `is` from `S.issues` and inserts two new tickets at the same index, each from `nextId()`. Part complexities come from `RESEARCH_SPLIT = {4:[2,3], 5:[3,3]}`. For part complexity c with sibling total T: `base = BASE[c] × R(.85,1.15)`, `kpi = round(is.kpi × c ÷ T)` for the first part and `is.kpi − first` for the second; outsourced `pay` splits the same way. Copied fields: `stack`, `client`, `sens`, `due`, `out`, `store`, `inc` (always false). `big` is kept only when the part's complexity is ≥3. Reset fields: `trap=false`, `trueCx=c`, `trueBase=base`, `revealed=false`, `evaluated=false`, `rescoped=false`, `merge=false`, `research=false`, `tries=0`. After splitting, `sel.issue` points to the first part. Each part stores `from` (the original's id); the overdue check in `endDay` drops trust only once per `from` on the same day, so splitting never makes a late ticket cost more trust (found in `/spectra-review`; the user chose once per original over documenting a double penalty).

Alternative considered: mutate the original into part 1 and add part 2 — rejected, two fresh ids keep logs and analytics unambiguous.

### Merge conflict clears research

The merge-conflict rewrite in `settle` also sets `research=false`, so the follow-up ticket of a directly dispatched research ticket is a normal ticket.

### Analytics and log

`dispatch` and `job_result` add `research` (boolean, the ticket's flag at dispatch). New event `research` after the action's result is known: `via` (`agent`／`self`), `vendor`, `model`, `bill` (`none` for self), `cx`, `stack`, `gig`, `outcome` (`split`, or `quota` on a shortfall). Refused actions send nothing. Log lines:

- `✂ <title>｜研究｜<agent> / <model>・<bill>｜拆成「<a>」（複雜度 N）＋「<b>」（複雜度 N）｜<k> tokens｜<spend>｜<h>h`
- `✂ <title>｜自己研究｜拆成「<a>」（複雜度 N）＋「<b>」（複雜度 N）｜<h>h`
- `✗ <title>｜研究｜<agent> / <model>・<bill>｜額度不夠，研究沒做完｜<spend>`
- The dispatch decision line tags include 需研究 for a research ticket.

### Values tuned after the simulator run

Supersedes the first values (direct ×2.5, agent research 80k and 1h, self research 3h). Measured 2026-10-10 with `SIM_N=100`, seeds 101 and 202: with the first values the research-first auto-player was −3.3%～+2.0% against the direct-dispatch player in parallel mode (most cells negative) because the default player mostly dispatches DeepSeek Chat at NT$0.03 per 1k tokens, so ×2.5 tokens barely cost it anything while research cost hours; self research was −9%～−12% against no research tickets. Variants measured on seed 101: ×4 alone, cheaper research alone, and both. The user chose both: `RESEARCH_DIRECT_TK=4`, `RESEARCH_TK=40`, `RESEARCH_HRS=0.5`, `RESEARCH_SELF_HRS=1.5`. With these values, in parallel mode the research-first player beats the direct player in every cell for both seeds (+0.1%～+3.6%) and stays within −1.1%～−2.8% of a game without research tickets. The full table is in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

### Card and rules modal

The card shows a 需研究 chip and, on a research ticket, the hint line 直接派工 token ×4（先研究可拆成兩張小單）. The rules modal 工單與陷阱 tab adds a research-ticket section with rate, split table, direct premium and both research costs interpolated from the constants.

### Simulator switches

`SIM_RESEARCH_RATE=<rate>` overrides `RESEARCH_RATE` via `setResearchRate`. `SIM_RESEARCH=agent|self` makes the auto-player research a research ticket before dispatching it (agent: with the vendor, model and billing it would have dispatched with; self: by itself) when research is allowed; otherwise it dispatches directly as before.

## Implementation Contract

**Observable behavior:**

- A research ticket card shows 需研究 and the ×4 hint; the dispatch panel shows two research buttons with their cost and no evaluation button.
- The panel's token range and cost line for a research ticket are 4 × the same ticket without the flag.
- After agent or self research, the queue has two new tickets with the split complexities, KPI summing to the original, the same due date, and the original is gone.
- Analytics events and log lines as listed under Analytics and log.

**Data shape:** ticket gains `research: boolean` and `parts: [string, string]` (only on research tickets). Older saves lack both; `!is.research` treats them as normal tickets. `SAVE_VER` unchanged.

**Failure modes:** a refused research action changes nothing and sends nothing; an agent research with a quota shortfall charges what it used, spends the hours, does not split, and sends `research` with `outcome` `quota`.

**Acceptance:** `node tools/check.js` exits 0 with assertions for every `research-tickets` scenario and the modified requirements; `SIM_RESEARCH_RATE=0 SIM_SEED=7 SIM_N=10 node tools/sim.js` output is identical before and after the change; `SIM_RESEARCH=agent` measurements recorded in `docs/DESIGN.md`.

**In scope:** generation, premium, both research actions, splitting, card and panel UI, analytics, log, rules modal, simulator switches, check assertions, DESIGN.md.

**Out of scope:** reasoning-effort or investment effects on research, recursive splitting, preset or batch changes, score formula.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Rate 0.3 and the research costs are estimates] → `SIM_RESEARCH=agent` vs `SIM_RESEARCH_RATE=0` (parallel target −5%～+5%) and `SIM_RESEARCH=agent` vs default (should be higher in most parallel cells); tune `RESEARCH_RATE`, `RESEARCH_DIRECT_TK`, `RESEARCH_TK` or `RESEARCH_SELF_HRS` if outside, and record the runs.
- [Splitting a complexity-5 ticket into 3＋3 costs more total base tokens (≈700k vs 850k ×4)] → intended: research is the efficient path; the simulator shows whether it is too efficient.
- [The default auto-player never researches, so default runs get worse] → expected; documented in DESIGN.md as a naive-player measurement.
- [Fixed-seed outputs change when the rate is non-zero] → the roll is skipped at rate 0, so equivalence can still be checked with `SIM_RESEARCH_RATE=0`.
